# Responsible AI

Ratas runs AI agents that act on your behalf — building, deploying, and
operating software. That power comes with responsibility. These are the
principles we design the platform around.

## Human oversight

Agents propose and act, but you stay in control. High-impact actions
(deploying, deleting data, spending, changing access) are surfaced for your
review rather than run silently. You can inspect what an agent did and undo
reversible actions.

## Transparency

- Agents keep an auditable trace of the steps they take and the tools they call.
- We're clear about what is automated and what needs your confirmation.
- Generated output is identifiable as agent-produced.

## Safety & guardrails

- Agents operate inside defined permissions and scopes — least privilege by
  default.
- Destructive or irreversible operations require explicit confirmation.
- We decline requests that enable harm, abuse, or illegal activity.

## Privacy & data

- Your data is used to run your agents — not to train shared public models
  without your consent.
- We collect the minimum needed to deliver the service. See our
  [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
- You control your data and can export or delete it.

## Limitations

AI agents can make mistakes, misread intent, or produce incorrect output.
Ratas is a tool, not a decision-maker. Review agent output before relying on it
for anything consequential — especially legal, financial, medical, or safety
matters.

## Accountability

- You are responsible for how you use agents and for the actions you authorize.
- We are responsible for building the guardrails, controls, and transparency
  that make responsible use possible.

## Reporting a concern

Found misuse, a safety issue, or unexpected behaviour? Tell us:
**admin@ratas.ai**
