# Privacy Policy

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This is a placeholder structure, not a binding
> legal document. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer and adjust to the data
> you actually process before launch. Last updated: _TBD_.

## 1. What we collect

- **Email address** — when you join the waitlist.
- **Usage analytics** — aggregate, privacy-first metrics via Cloudflare Web
  Analytics (no cookies, no cross-site tracking).
- **Chatbot data** _(when enabled)_ — messages you send to the assistant, and a
  first-party cookie used only to remember your session.

## 2. How we use it

- To **operate the waitlist** and notify you about early access and launch.
- To **provide and run the Service** — including executing your agents and
  storing the snippets, widgets, gadgets, and workflows you create.
- To **improve the product** using aggregate, non-identifying analytics.
- To **respond to you** and provide support.
- To **keep the Service safe** — detecting abuse, fraud, and security issues.

We do **not** sell your data, and we do not use your content to train shared
public AI models without your consent.

## 3. Cookies

Our analytics are **cookie-free** (Cloudflare Web Analytics). The chatbot, when
enabled, sets a single **first-party session cookie** only to remember a
returning visitor within a session — not to track you across sites or build an
advertising profile.

## 4. Third parties

We rely on a small set of processors, each handling only what their function
requires:

- **Cloudflare** — hosting, the AI runtime, and privacy-first analytics. Workers
  AI commits to no training on your content and zero data retention for standard
  inference ([data usage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/)).
- **AWS** — cloud infrastructure for certain backend services and storage.
- **Stripe / PayOS** — payment processing (when billing launches). We do not
  store full card details.

Your agents may also connect to third-party services **you** choose; data shared
with those is governed by their policies.

## 5. Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as
required by law. Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or the waitlist
closes; account and content data are kept while your account is active; chatbot
history is kept for a limited period. You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export,
or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.
To exercise any of these, email **admin@ratas.ai** and we will respond within a
reasonable, legally required timeframe.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and legal requirements evolve. Material
changes will be communicated through the Service or by email, and the "last
updated" date will change.

## 8. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: **admin@ratas.ai**
